# Wag and Tails Marketplace 🐾
A comprehensive e-commerce platform for all your pet care needs. Wag and Tails Marketplace offers a wide variety of products for pet lovers, including toys, accessories, and grooming essentials.

## Features 🚀
Browse Products: A user-friendly interface to explore a wide range of pet care products.
- Add to Cart: Seamless shopping experience with the ability to add items to the cart.
- Cart Management: View, update, and manage your cart items.
- Responsive Design: Optimized for desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
- Simple Navigation: Easy access to product pages, cart, and other essential features.
